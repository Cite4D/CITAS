## Background & Objectives

The **International Conference on Innovation, Technology and Analytics for Society (CITAS 2027)** is a three-day academic conference to be held on **February 16 to 18, 2027** at De La Salle University, Manila, Philippines.

The conference brings together faculty, researchers, students, and representatives of partner institutions to share work on innovation, technology, and analytics and their direct application to society. It also serves as a premier venue for undergraduate and graduate capstone projects to present their research to an external audience.

### Conference Objectives:
1. Provide a forum for the presentation and discussion of research and applied work in innovation, technology, and analytics.
2. Strengthen linkages with local and international academic, government, and industry partners.
3. Give students (BSIT / BSIS / MSIT) capstone and research teams the opportunity to present before invited guests and professionals.
4. Host visiting guests through a welcome dinner and technical workshop.

**Publication Note:** CITAS 2027 handles full papers, extended abstracts, and posters with possible publication in the **Philippine Computing Journal** in cooperation with editor **Mr. Nathaniel Oco**.

## Paper Submission Guidelines

Submissions should describe unpublished completed original work. Authors intending to submit should follow the **two-column ACL format (A4 size)** and may consist of **up to six (6) pages of content**, excluding references and appendices.

### Formatting Templates:
- **LaTeX Style Files:** [https://github.com/acl-org/acl-style-files](https://github.com/acl-org/acl-style-files)
- **Microsoft Word (.docx):** [https://2023.aclweb.org/calls/style_and_formatting/](https://2023.aclweb.org/calls/style_and_formatting/)

### Review Policy:
- No page numbers should appear in the paper.
- Submissions will be judged based on relevance, technical strength, significance, and opportunities of interest to attendees.
- Reviewing is **double-blind**; authors must not indicate their names and affiliations in the submitted papers.
- Accepted papers will be presented orally or as posters as determined by the committee.

### Online Submission Form:
- Papers must be submitted online through the official conference form:  
  **Submission Form (Google Forms):** [https://forms.gle/zcJwr1Lb78QzjysG8](https://forms.gle/zcJwr1Lb78QzjysG8)

## How to Enable GitHub Pages Backup

This repository comes pre-configured with two ways to host the live backup website for free on GitHub:

### Option 1: GitHub Actions (Recommended)
1. In your GitHub repository, go to **Settings** &rarr; **Pages**.
2. Under **Build and deployment** &rarr; **Source**, select **GitHub Actions**.
3. Every time you push to `main` or `master`, the workflow in `.github/workflows/deploy-gh-pages.yml` automatically builds and publishes the website to `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/`.

### Option 2: Deploy from `/docs` Folder (Instant)
1. In GitHub repository **Settings** &rarr; **Pages**.
2. Under **Build and deployment** &rarr; **Source**, select **Deploy from a branch**.
3. Select your branch (`master` or `main`) and folder `/docs`, then click **Save**.
4. The site will be live immediately without needing any workflow runs.
